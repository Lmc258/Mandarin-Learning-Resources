## 🧠 Study Method

A general framework for learning Mandarin effectively. The key is layering **passive input** with **active recall** — not just consuming content, but forcing your brain to produce the language.

### Step 1 — Get the Fundamentals (Beginner)

Before anything else, get a basic foundation in place:

- **Learn Pinyin** — the romanisation system for Mandarin pronunciation. Essential before you touch characters.
- **Learn the 4 tones** — mispronouncing tones changes the meaning entirely. Get these in early.
- **Start character recognition** — use Mandarin Blueprint and/or Anki flashcard decks to build a character base. Active flashcard study (spaced repetition) is the most efficient method.

### Step 2 — Build Vocabulary Actively

Once you have basics, start accumulating words and phrases deliberately:

- Pull from a **frequency word list** — the most common 1,000 words cover the vast majority of everyday speech. Start there.
  - 📋 [Mandarin Frequency List 1–1000 (Wiktionary)](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Appendix:Mandarin_Frequency_lists/1-1000)
- Add words to **Anki** and review daily. Short daily sessions beat long infrequent ones.
- Learn **phrases and sentences**, not just isolated words — context helps retention.

### Step 3 — Comprehensible Input (Passive → Active Listening)

This is sometimes called *comprehensible input* — listening/reading to material that is just slightly above your current level:

- Watch **movies, vlogs, and podcasts** with subtitles showing Pinyin, characters, and your native language translation simultaneously (Language Reactor is great for this).
- Listen to **translated audio** on repeat — e.g. record yourself talking through your day in English, translate it to Mandarin using AI, then listen to that audio repeatedly.
  > ⚠️ *Still need to try this out personally — see the [30-min routine video](https://youtu.be/_wYjXdt4a2I?si=cQngb9h54ylCF4KK) for the method explained in detail.*
- **Shadowing** — listen to a native phrase and repeat it immediately, mimicking tone and rhythm. This trains your mouth as much as your brain.

### Step 4 — Active Recall & Sentence Production

The hardest and most important step. Consuming input isn't enough — you need to produce the language from scratch:

- **Respond to your day in Mandarin** — narrate what you're doing, what you see, what you're thinking. Even internally.
- Practice **constructing sentences from nothing**, not just repeating memorised phrases.
- Use AI tools (e.g. ChatGPT, Claude) to check your sentences, get corrections, or generate practice prompts.

### Summary

```
Pinyin + Tones → Flashcards (characters/vocab) → Comprehensible Input (listen/watch) → Shadowing → Active Recall (speak/produce)
```

Consistency is everything. 30 focused minutes daily will outpace 3-hour weekend sessions.

## 📅 Daily Study Plans

These are flexible templates based on the study method above. Pick the one that fits your day — doing a 15-min plan consistently beats doing a 90-min plan twice a week.

> All plans follow the same priority order: **Active study first, passive input second.** Anki and sentence work take real focus; listening can happen while doing other things.

---

### ⚡ 15 Minutes — Bare Minimum / Busy Day

*Don't break the streak. This is your "no excuses" plan.*

| Time | Activity | What to do |
|------|----------|------------|
| 10 min | 🃏 Anki flashcards | Review due cards only — do not add new ones if pressed for time |
| 5 min | 🗣️ Active recall | Narrate something from your day out loud in Mandarin — even just a few sentences |

**Goal:** Keep the habit alive. Momentum matters more than volume on bad days.

---

### 🕐 30 Minutes — Solid Daily Session

*The sweet spot for sustainable daily progress.*

| Time | Activity | What to do |
|------|----------|------------|
| 10 min | 🃏 Anki flashcards | Review due cards + add 5–10 new words from the frequency list |
| 10 min | 📖 Structured study | Mandarin Blueprint lesson, or review a grammar point via Yoyo Chinese |
| 5 min | 🔊 Shadowing | Pick one ChinesePod dialogue or short clip — listen then repeat immediately |
| 5 min | 🗣️ Active recall | Respond to something from your day in Mandarin; write or speak a few original sentences |

**Goal:** Balance all four pillars — vocab, structure, listening, and production.

---

### 🕐 60 Minutes — Full Session

*Use this on days when you have real time to invest.*

| Time | Activity | What to do |
|------|----------|------------|
| 10 min | 🃏 Anki flashcards | Review all due cards + add 10–15 new words |
| 15 min | 📖 Structured study | Mandarin Blueprint lesson or character writing practice |
| 15 min | 🎧 Comprehensible input | Watch a short video/vlog with Language Reactor (Pinyin + translation subtitles on) |
| 10 min | 🔊 Shadowing | Shadow the audio from what you just watched — replay and repeat key lines |
| 10 min | 🗣️ Active recall & production | Narrate your day, write a short paragraph, or use AI (Claude/ChatGPT) to check sentences you've constructed |

**Goal:** Hit every layer of the method. You should leave this session having produced language, not just consumed it.

---

### 🕑 90 Minutes — Deep Dive / Weekend Session

*For when you want to make a real dent. Don't try to do this daily — burnout is real.*

| Time | Activity | What to do |
|------|----------|------------|
| 10 min | 🃏 Anki flashcards | Full review + add 15–20 new words |
| 20 min | 📖 Structured study | Mandarin Blueprint — aim to complete a full lesson or character set |
| 20 min | 🎧 Comprehensible input | Watch a longer video or podcast episode with subtitles via Language Reactor |
| 15 min | 🔊 Shadowing | Replay key segments and shadow intensively — focus on tones and rhythm |
| 15 min | ✍️ Self-talk audio prep | Record yourself talking through your day in English, translate to Mandarin with AI, save and listen on repeat |
| 10 min | 🗣️ Active recall | Construct 5–10 original sentences without looking anything up. Check with AI afterwards. |

**Goal:** Push into uncomfortable territory — produce sentences you haven't rehearsed, encounter words you don't know yet.

---

### 🎧 Passive Listening — Anytime (Commute, Gym, Chores)

*Not a replacement for active study — a bonus layer on top.*

- Loop your translated self-talk audio
- Play ChinesePod "Newbie" or "Elementary" episodes in the background
- Leave Mandarin music or slow-speech content playing during low-focus tasks

> Passive listening alone won't make you fluent, but it builds familiarity with tones, rhythm, and common phrases over time.

## 💡 Tips & Notes

A place to jot down learning tips, personal notes, and things that clicked.

- **Tones matter** — Mandarin has 4 tones + a neutral tone; getting these right early makes a big difference.
- **Learn Pinyin first** — it's the foundation for pronunciation before you tackle characters.
- **Consistency beats intensity** — short daily sessions beat long infrequent ones, especially for character memorisation.
- **Active study over passive** — flashcards and sentence production beat just watching videos. Both matter, but don't let passive input replace active recall.
- **Shadowing works** — repeat phrases immediately after hearing them, matching tone and rhythm. Feels awkward, works well.
- **Comprehensible input** — content just above your current level is the sweet spot; too easy = no growth, too hard = you tune out.
- *Add your own tips here as you learn!*
